## Source-first proof

Trace the concrete actor-controlled input through intervening controls to the
sensitive operation and terminal attacker effect. Inspect callers and downstream
consumers, denial/fallback/error paths, representations, lifetime and validation
versus use. Preserve actor/tenant/resource/configuration compatibility. Explicitly
inspect the strongest contrary control and explain why it prevents or fails to
prevent the assigned route. A safe sibling is not proof another operation is safe.
A baseline root alone is not additional impact. No-evidence repetition is redundant.

Use only packet-listed accepted artifacts, the complete authorized linked baseline
when applicable, and the exact assigned source view. Never consume unpublished
peer output. Do not expand source access or substitute base for derived code.
Use registered graph questions only through run-codegraph.sh with phase
gap-exploration and your stable task_id. Retain valid graph_receipt_refs and exact
limitations; navigate back to source. A graph edge, absent edge, installed backend
or raw receipt cannot establish a candidate. Source-only investigation is useful
when graph setup/query fails. External deployment facts stay explicit missing
prerequisites. No target execution, network, dynamic reproduction or borrowed
experiment capacity.

## Bounded result draft

Read schemas/v2/gap-result.schema.json and gap-common.schema.json. Write only
`gap-exploration/drafts/<task-id>/result.json`, never an admitted result, dispatch,
plan, packet, phase manifest, upstream record or report. The coordinator admits
your draft after actual terminal delivery. Include exact envelope, assigned
identity/source_view, attempt and provenance. Provenance is actor,selector,
artifact_ref=`gap-exploration/results/<task-id>.json`; linked adds execution_id.
Omit `admission`: its packet binding and source/support receipts are generated by
the publisher, never authored or copied by a worker.

Give resolution outcome/reason/missing_fact/next_action/source_refs. Outcomes are
established, contradicted, locally_unresolved, externally_blocked, budget_deferred,
execution_failed, redundant. Established and contradicted require inspected source
line references. Unresolved outcomes require exact missing fact and next action,
not clean coverage. Include graph_receipt_refs and limitations arrays.

At most TWO candidates per worker. IDs are `GC-<task-id>-<local-id>`. Each candidate
has classification (new_root,new_chain,impact_expansion,baseline_correction,
baseline_revalidation), baseline_ids, root_cause_key, terminal_impact, reason,
evidence_refs, source_locations [{file,line}], objective_ids=[assigned objective],
question_id, source_view, conditions, ordered causal_trace [{step,source_refs}],
inspected_controls [{control,effect,source_refs}], and proof obligations:
actor_control,reachability,security_boundary,impact,intervening_controls,
conditions_compatible. Every obligation needs outcome=established, substantive
reason and exact inspected source_refs. Missing essential proof is NOT a candidate;
retain it in resolution with the actual next action. Source refs use canonical
repository-prefixed relative paths with real line ranges. Do not paste secrets.
The helper adds durable provenance/contributors/artifact_ref/assessment context;
never stamp another producer's route or claim independent verification.

Stay inside the assigned proof obligation and stop condition. Additional branches
remain explicit limitations/leads, not recursively dispatched work. Return strict
status,task_id,artifacts,warnings,errors containing the actual draft path. A failed
execution cannot masquerade as an empty successful result; messages and file
presence are not terminal completion. Do not claim a new retry entitlement.
