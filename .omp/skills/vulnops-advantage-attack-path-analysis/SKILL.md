# Advantage Attack Path Analysis

Read the [native Advantage context](../vulnops-advantage-security-scan/references/advantage-context.md)
first. When reconstructing or challenging a capability path, read
[attack-path analysis](references/attack-path-analysis.md).

## Bound input and responsibility

The native investigator receives the assigned objective/question, candidate
validation, source anchors, full baseline access, original counterevidence and
status, attacker starting position, and exact missing proof. Synthesis may use
this method to build a dossier; the fresh verifier challenges it independently.
Read source and admitted evidence only. No credential use, direct reproduction,
policy edits, recursive scan, or independent final report is authorized.

## Method and calibration

Establish the actual starting access, source-backed exposure and ordered
capability transfers, challenge compatible conditions and counterevidence,
then explain the supported delta beyond inherited impact. Inherited primitive
proof is not composition proof. A contradicted or unresolved edge cannot support
its dependent chain; independently supported components remain eligible.

For reportability apply `skill://vulnops-exclusion-rules` and
`skill://vulnops-self-verification`; for severity calibration read
`skill://vulnops-severity-guidance`. Keep evidence confidence separate from impact.
Existing policy is read-only contextual evidence, not suppression authority.

## Native output

Preserve transition assessments in the result's candidate `reason` and cited
evidence, then populate the synthesis dossier's ordered `primitive_steps` and
`transitions` where applicable. Retain precise root-control/affected locations,
baseline references and relation, conditions, source-only versus observed proof,
impact limits, strongest counterevidence, and full contributor union. Keep
unresolved edges and exact next proof in terminal question accounting. An
objective is not achieved merely because an intermediate capability was
established; fresh final verification must support its exact success condition.
